About

Architecture: ViT image encoder → projector → causal transformer decoder (LLaVA-style prefix-LM), trained from scratch on procedurally generated skeletons. Real photos go through on-device pose estimation (MediaPipe) and are drawn as the same colour-coded skeleton before the VLM reads them. Red = subject's right, green = left, blue = trunk.

Research and education demo, not a medical device. Do not use it for diagnosis or treatment decisions.
