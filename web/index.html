About

Architecture: CNN-ViT image encoder (with CoordConv) → projector → causal transformer decoder (LLaVA-style prefix-LM), trained from scratch on procedurally generated skeletons. Real photos go through on-device pose estimation (MediaPipe) and are drawn as the same colour-coded skeleton before the VLM reads them. Body skeleton: red = subject's right, green = left, blue = trunk. Hands are put into a standard orientation and drawn from three sides (red = front, green = side, blue = top), so the model can read finger curl in 3D. Wrists add the forearm (from body tracking) as a reference line.

Clinicians and researchers: collect goniometer-labelled data to fine-tune these models on real patients.

Research and education demo, not a medical device. Do not use it for diagnosis or treatment decisions.
